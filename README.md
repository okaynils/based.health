# based.health - Open Source Health Protocols

**based.health** is a community-managed, open-source archive of health protocols designed to be simple enough to implement today and rigorous enough to deserve trust.

In an era of scattered podcast information, marketing-driven blogs, and hidden affiliate agendas, **based.health** keeps the useful part: short protocols, explicit caveats, and references. No ads. No fluff. No medical heroics.

## The Philosophy

Health information is currently fragmented and high-friction. We believe that human optimization standards should be:
- **Open Source:** Owned by the community, not a corporation.
- **Evidence-Based:** Meaningful claims need credible references and visible uncertainty.
- **Minimalist:** Protocols start with what to do, not a long article.
- **Safety-First:** Contraindications are part of the protocol, not an afterthought.
- **Low-Friction:** The starter kit favors free, low-risk lifestyle defaults before advanced interventions.

## Current Scope

The first version focuses on low-risk lifestyle protocols:
- sleep and circadian rhythm
- walking and basic activity
- resistance training
- simple nutrition defaults
- hydration

Supplements, medical-condition-specific advice, medications, hormone protocols, extreme diets, and high-risk interventions are out of scope until the review model is stronger.

## Protocol Format

Every protocol should answer, in order:
1. What do I do?
2. How often, how long, and what does it cost?
3. Why might it work?
4. Who should avoid or modify it?
5. What evidence supports it?
