### What to do
Add a clear protein anchor to your first meal.

1. Pick one source: eggs, Greek yogurt, tofu, tempeh, fish, meat, cottage cheese, legumes, or protein powder.
2. Aim for roughly 25-40 g protein in the meal.
3. Keep the rest simple: fruit, vegetables, oats, potatoes, rice, or sourdough.
4. Repeat the same default meal on busy days.

### Why it works
Protein supports muscle protein synthesis and satiety. Getting a meaningful dose early reduces the chance of trying to back-load your whole protein target at night.

### Modify or avoid
If you have kidney disease or another condition with protein restrictions, follow your care plan. If appetite is low in the morning, use the first meal you actually eat.

### Evidence
Protein needs rise with resistance training and lean-mass goals.[^1] A meta-analysis found that gains from protein supplementation plateaued around 1.6 g/kg/day for resistance training outcomes, though individual needs vary.[^2]

[^1]: [Lemon (1998): Effects of exercise on dietary protein requirements](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9841962/)
[^2]: [Morton et al. (2018): Protein supplementation and resistance training meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28698222/)
