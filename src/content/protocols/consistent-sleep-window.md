### What to do
Create a nightly sleep window that gives you enough opportunity to sleep.

1. Pick a wake time first.
2. Count back 8 hours.
3. Start a 30-minute wind-down before that.
4. Keep the window within 60 minutes on weekends.
5. If you cannot sleep, fix wake time, morning light, caffeine, and evening light before adding gadgets.

### Why it works
Most adults need at least 7 hours of sleep. A consistent window gives your body a repeated chance to hit that target.

### Modify or avoid
If you snore loudly, stop breathing during sleep, wake gasping, have severe daytime sleepiness, or cannot sleep despite good opportunity, get evaluated. Protocols do not replace diagnosis.

### Evidence
The American Academy of Sleep Medicine and Sleep Research Society recommend 7 or more hours of sleep for healthy adults on a regular basis.[^1] Public health guidance also emphasizes sleep duration and regular schedule as basic sleep health behaviors.[^2]
