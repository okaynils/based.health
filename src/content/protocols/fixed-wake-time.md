### What to do
Pick one wake time and hold it within a 30-minute window, including weekends.

1. Choose a wake time that allows at least 7 hours in bed.
2. Set one alarm for that time.
3. Get out of bed within 10 minutes.
4. Pair it with morning light.
5. If sleep was short, protect the next night's bedtime instead of sleeping late.

### Why it works
A stable wake time anchors the day. It makes light exposure, caffeine timing, meals, training, and sleep pressure more predictable.

Bedtime is easier to adjust after wake time is stable. If wake time moves by hours, the whole system moves with it.

### Modify or avoid
If you do shift work, have insomnia, bipolar disorder, sleep apnea, or another diagnosed sleep condition, use a clinician-guided plan. If you are sleep deprived, prioritize enough sleep over forcing an aggressive wake time.

### Evidence
Sleep organizations recommend regular sleep schedules, and adult consensus guidance recommends at least 7 hours of sleep on a regular basis.[^1][^2] Regularity is a practical lever, but it should not override total sleep need.
