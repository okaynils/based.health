### What to do
Do two full-body sessions per week.

1. Pick 4 movements: squat, hinge, push, pull.
2. Do 2-3 sets of 5-12 reps each.
3. Stop each set with 1-3 good reps still in reserve.
4. Add reps first, then weight.
5. Keep at least one rest day between sessions.

### Why it works
Muscle is useful tissue. Strength training helps preserve it, improves function, and gives the body a reason to keep adapting.

### Modify or avoid
Use bodyweight, machines, bands, dumbbells, or barbells. The tool matters less than progressive, controlled effort. Pain that changes your movement is a signal to modify.

### Evidence
The CDC recommends muscle-strengthening activity for all major muscle groups at least 2 days per week.[^1] Resistance training is also a core intervention in exercise and sports nutrition guidance for improving strength and lean mass.[^2]
