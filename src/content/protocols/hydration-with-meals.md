### What to do
Drink water with each meal and after waking.

1. Put a glass of water next to breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
2. Drink to thirst between meals.
3. Use urine color as a rough sanity check, not an obsession.
4. Add electrolytes only when sweat, heat, long exercise, or medical guidance calls for it.

### Why it works
Hydration needs vary by body size, diet, heat, altitude, activity, and sweat rate. Meal pairing is simple because it turns hydration into an environmental default.

### Modify or avoid
If you are on fluid restriction or have kidney, heart, or sodium-balance issues, do not increase fluid intake without medical guidance.

### Evidence
The National Academies found no single water intake level fits all healthy adults because needs vary widely, and thirst plus usual beverage intake can maintain hydration for many people.[^1] This protocol uses that variability instead of pretending everyone needs the same bottle target.

[^1]: [National Academies: Dietary Reference Intakes for water](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/10925/chapter/6)
